# Storyboard: "5 free AI tools trending this week"

Format: 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, about 40 s. Built in Remotion, voice from ElevenLabs (owner's cloned voice), finished with FFmpeg. No avatar.

## Look

- **Colours:** brand yellow `#FFD400`, ink black `#141518`, white `#FFFFFF` for body text. Small accents only: red `#FF4D4D` for "bug/hole", green `#3DDC84` for "fixed".
- **Fonts:** Anton (big kinetic headlines, uppercase), Inter (body and captions).
- **Logo:** the yellow circle with black speech bubble and "AI", redrawn as clean vector (no pencil icon, no glow). Shown on the hook and the end card.
- **Background:** near-black with a faint yellow dot grid and soft yellow glow that drifts slowly.
- **Progress bar:** 5 small pills at the top; the current tool's pill fills yellow.
- **Transitions:** a diagonal yellow panel sweeps across between scenes (about 0.25 s), with a soft whoosh.
- **Icons:** generic line icons (Phosphor) only. No Chrome, Google, OpenAI, Block or GitHub logos anywhere; tool names appear as plain text.

## Captions

Word-by-word captions in the lower third (above Instagram's bottom UI area), 2–4 words at a time. White Inter Bold on a black rounded pill; the word being spoken turns yellow and pops slightly. Timings come from ElevenLabs' character timestamps, so they match the voice exactly.

## Scenes

Times are targets; final cuts follow the actual voiceover.

### 1. Hook (0–4 s)
- VO: "Five free AI tools developers are grabbing on GitHub this week. Number three is from OpenAI."
- Huge yellow **5** slams in from the camera with a small screen shake.
- **FREE AI TOOLS** stamps in word by word beside it; "trending this week" types on underneath.
- On "Number three…": a yellow tag drops in, **#3 is from OpenAI 👀**.
- Logo sits small in the top corner.

### 2. Chrome DevTools MCP (4–10 s)
- VO: "One. Chrome DevTools MCP. It lets your AI coding agent open a real Chrome browser, click around, and find what's broken."
- Big outline **01** slides in from the left; tool name types on letter by letter in yellow.
- Animation: a plain browser window (no Chrome logo) opens; a robot cursor clicks two buttons; a red bug marker appears on a broken element and gets circled.
- Text card: **Your AI agent controls a real browser**, with "real browser" on a yellow highlight that wipes in.

### 3. Plannotator (10–16 s)
- VO: "Two. Plannotator. Before your AI writes any code, you read its plan and mark it up, like correcting homework."
- **02** + name.
- Animation: a "PLAN" sheet with 5 lines; a yellow highlighter swipes one line, a red pen strikes another and adds a comment bubble "change this", then a big tick stamp.
- Text card: **Review your AI's plan before it builds**.

### 4. Codex Security (16–22 s)
- VO: "Three. Codex Security from OpenAI. It scans your code, finds security holes, and writes the fix."
- **03** + name, small grey label "by OpenAI" (text only).
- Animation: lines of code; a yellow scan bar sweeps down; one line flashes red with a broken-lock icon; it rewrites itself and turns green with a shield tick.
- Text card: **Finds and fixes security holes**.
- Small footnote: "Runs in Codex. Needs an OpenAI account."

### 5. Modern Web Guidance (22–28 s)
- VO: "Four. Modern Web Guidance, backed by the Chrome team. It teaches your AI agent today's web standards, so it stops writing outdated code."
- **04** + name.
- Animation: an "OLD CODE" card with a dusty, faded look gets a red X and slides away; a fresh "2026 STANDARDS" card slides in with a book icon and sparkle.
- Text card: **No more outdated code from AI**.

### 6. Buzz (28–34 s)
- VO: "Five. Buzz, from Block. One workspace where your team and your AI agents work in the same rooms."
- **05** + name, small grey label "by Block" (text only).
- Animation: a chat room panel; avatars pop in alternately as people (initial circles) and AI agents (robot icons); message bubbles stack up from both.
- Text card: **People + AI agents in one workspace**.

### 7. Call to action (34–40 s)
- VO: "Want all five links in one place? Comment TOOLS and I'll send them to your DM."
- A comment box slides up; **TOOLS** types into it; send button taps; a paper-plane flies to a DM icon that gets a red "1" badge.
- Big headline: **COMMENT "TOOLS"**, subline **get all 5 links in your DM**.
- Last second: logo end card with "Save this ⤵ Follow for daily AI tools".

## Sound

- Voice: owner's ElevenLabs clone, `eleven_multilingual_v2`, read as one take with timestamps.
- SFX: whoosh on scene transitions, soft pop on text stamps, click on the CTA send button.
- Music: optional low background bed, ducked under the voice.
