# Storyboard: "ChatGPT can now shop and pay for you" (ChatGPT Wallet)

Format: 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, about 41 s. Built in Remotion, voice from ElevenLabs (owner's cloned voice), finished with FFmpeg. No avatar. Script and facts: `posts_2026-11-14.md`, Post 1.

## Look (same as the "5 free AI tools" reel)

- **Colours:** brand yellow `#FFD400`, ink black `#141518`, white body text. Accents: green `#3DDC84` for "approved / safe", red `#FF4D4D` for "the catch".
- **Fonts:** Anton (big uppercase headlines), Inter (body and captions).
- **Logo:** our yellow "AI" bubble on the hook and end card.
- **Background:** near-black, faint yellow dot grid, slow drifting yellow glow.
- **Progress bar:** 7 small pills at the top, one per scene.
- **Transitions:** diagonal yellow panel sweep (about 0.25 s) with a soft whoosh.
- **Brands:** OpenAI, ChatGPT, Visa, Mastercard and American Express appear as plain text only. No logos, no card-network colours or shapes. The payment card is a generic black-and-yellow card with "•••• 4821". The phone is a plain rounded rectangle, not any real phone.

## Captions

Word-by-word captions in the lower third, 2–4 words at a time. White Inter Bold on a black pill; the spoken word turns yellow. Timings from ElevenLabs timestamps.

## Scenes

Times are targets; final cuts follow the real voiceover.

### 1. Hook (0–4 s)
- VO: "ChatGPT can now buy things for you. And the AI never sees your real card number."
- A shopping cart icon slams in with a small screen shake.
- **CHATGPT CAN NOW SHOP AND PAY FOR YOU** stamps in word by word, "PAY" on a yellow highlight.
- On "never sees your real card number": the number on a card scrambles into ••••.

### 2. What it is (4–10 s)
- VO: "OpenAI just launched ChatGPT Wallet. You add your card once, then ask your AI agent to find and buy what you need."
- Plain phone outline; a card slides into a wallet icon on screen with a "Saved ✓" tick.
- A chat bubble types: "Find me running shoes under $100". A robot cursor browses three product tiles and picks one.
- Text card: **ChatGPT Wallet: add your card once** ("OpenAI" in small grey text above).

### 3. You approve every purchase (10–17 s)
- VO: "Before it pays, you see the items, the total and the card. Then you approve with your face or fingerprint. Every single purchase."
- A checkout sheet slides up: item, total $89.00, card •••• 4821, each line ticking in.
- A face outline and a fingerprint icon pulse; the button turns green: **Approved**.
- Text card: **You approve every purchase with your face or fingerprint.**

### 4. The shop never gets your card (17–24 s)
- VO: "The shop gets a stand-in card number called a payment token, not your real card details. It is like handing a valet a spare key instead of your whole key ring."
- The card turns into a small yellow "TOKEN" card with a different number, which flies to a shop icon. The real card stays behind a lock.
- Analogy beat: a single spare key slides out of a full key ring and goes to the valet; the ring stays with you.
- Text card: **The shop never gets your real card number.**

### 5. What you keep (24–30 s)
- VO: "You still keep your card rewards, and you can still dispute a bad order. It was built with Visa, Mastercard and American Express."
- Two ticks appear one after another: **Card rewards ✓** and **Dispute protection ✓**.
- The three company names fade in as plain grey text on one line under "Built with".
- Text card: **Rewards and dispute protection stay.**

### 6. The catch (30–35 s)
- VO: "The catch: for now it is only for ChatGPT Pro users in the US, aged 18 and above, with a US-issued card."
- A red **THE CATCH** tag drops in.
- Three chips pop in: **US only** · **ChatGPT Pro** · **18+**.

### 7. Call to action (35–41 s)
- VO: "Own an online shop? Comment WALLET and I will send you what to change so AI agents can buy from you."
- A comment box slides up; **WALLET** types in; the send button taps; a paper plane flies to a DM icon with a red "1".
- Headline: **COMMENT "WALLET"**, subline **get the shop-owner checklist**.
- Last second: logo end card, "Save this ⤵ Follow for daily AI updates".

## Sound

- Voice: owner's ElevenLabs clone, `eleven_multilingual_v2`, one take with timestamps.
- SFX: whoosh on transitions, soft pop on text stamps, a "ding" on Approved, click on the CTA send button.
- Music: optional low bed, ducked under the voice.

## One change from the script

Scene 3 on-screen text says "face or fingerprint" instead of "Face ID", because Face ID is a brand name and the voiceover already says "face or fingerprint".
