# Storyboard: "One photo → a full 3D model" (Idol)

Format: 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, about 40 s. Built in Remotion, voice from ElevenLabs (owner's cloned voice). Script and facts: `posts_2026-11-14.md`, Post 2 (re-checked 14 Nov 2026).

## Style: "3D depth" (new; not panels, not a phone)

The first reel used flat panels with a diagonal wipe. The Wallet reel used a phone with camera moves. This one is about 3D, so the whole reel lives in 3D space:

- **The star is a dot-cloud object.** A simple chair (and later a mug) drawn from thousands of small glowing dots that turn in real 3D perspective. This is what a "Gaussian splat" looks like, so the picture explains the idea. It is our own drawing, not real Idol output, and the reel never claims it is.
- **The camera flies through layers.** Text, photo cards and the dot cloud sit at different depths. Moving between scenes, the camera glides forward through them (parallax) instead of cutting.
- **Transitions:** the dot cloud bursts into loose dots and the dots re-form into the next scene's shape.
- **Headlines:** big Anton words that tilt in 3D, as if printed on floating glass sheets, with yellow edges.
- **Captions:** one word at a time, large and centred low on the screen; each word flips up from below.
- **Colours:** brand yellow `#FFD400` and black. Dots are yellow-to-white with a soft glow.
- **Brands:** Hugging Face, NVIDIA and RTX 4090 as plain text only. No logos.

## Scenes

### 1. Hook (0–4 s)
- VO: "One photo in. A full 3D model out. In about 20 seconds."
- A flat photo card of a chair (drawn, flat colours) floats in, facing the camera.
- On "3D model out": the card tilts back, its picture breaks into dots, and the dots lift off and form a chair that starts turning in 3D.
- Headline on glass: **1 PHOTO → 3D MODEL**, and under it "in about 20 seconds".

### 2. What it is (4–10 s)
- VO: "This is Idol, a free AI model in the top ten trending on Hugging Face right now."
- Camera glides forward; the word **IDOL** rises in huge 3D letters made of the same dots.
- Glass tag: **Top 10 trending on Hugging Face** with a small "#7 on 14 Nov" note, plus a **FREE** badge.

### 3. How it works (10–17 s)
- VO: "Give it a single picture of an object. It removes the background and builds the whole thing in 3D, even the back side the photo never showed."
- A photo card of a mug on a cluttered background. On "removes the background", the clutter peels away like a sticker.
- The mug turns into dots and spins round to show its back; the hidden side lights up in yellow as it is "filled in".
- Glass text: **It builds the sides the photo never showed.**

### 4. The sculptor (17–24 s)
- VO: "Think of a sculptor who sees one photo of a chair and carves the full chair, guessing the back correctly."
- Split depth: a small photo pinned at the front left; behind it, a block of dots gets "carved" by a chisel icon until the chair appears. The camera orbits round to show the back.
- Glass text: **Like a sculptor working from one photo.**

### 5. What you need (24–30 s)
- VO: "It runs on a gaming PC with an NVIDIA graphics card. It needs about 7 GB of graphics memory, and takes about 20 seconds on a top gaming card."
- Three glass cards fly in one after another at different depths, each with a simple line icon:
  - **NVIDIA graphics card**
  - **About 7 GB of graphics memory**
  - **About 20 s on an RTX 4090**
- The chair keeps turning softly in the background.

### 6. Budget3D (30–35 s)
- VO: "There is also Budget3D, an unofficial community version built on Idol. It spends less effort on empty space, so it can run about twice as fast."
- Two dot chairs side by side, labelled Idol and Budget3D. The empty space around Idol's chair fills with many tiny grey dots; around Budget3D's chair the same space is a few big dots (empty space merged into coarse pieces).
- Glass tag: **Budget3D**, small grey "unofficial · community · built on Idol"; badge **ABOUT 2× FASTER**.

### 7. Call to action (35–40 s)
- VO: "Want the links and the setup steps? Comment 3D and I will send them to you."
- The dots swirl and form a giant **"3D"**, sitting at the front.
- Headline: **COMMENT "3D"**, subline **get the links and setup steps**.
- Last second: logo end card, "Save this ⤵ Follow for daily AI updates".
